Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026 • Poke Bowl & Fresh Fusion Restaurant

1. Information Collection and Use

Poke Bowl & Fresh Fusion values your personal privacy. On this demonstration website, contact and order enquiry forms operate strictly on client-side simulation. No personally identifiable information (including your name, email, or telephone number) is permanently stored on external remote database servers without explicit operational agreements.

2. Local Storage and Client Preferences

To provide a seamless browsing experience, our website uses your browser's standard localStorage API to persist two interface settings:

  • Theme Mode (poke_theme): Remembers whether you prefer Dark Mode or Light Mode across page visits.
  • Text Direction (poke_direction): Remembers your chosen layout direction (LTR or RTL).

These settings remain strictly stored within your local web browser and are never transmitted to advertising trackers or third parties.

3. Frontend Demonstration Notice

The Order Enquiry form available on contact.html and the Custom Bowl Builder on home-2.html are interactive frontend demonstration interfaces. Submitting forms does not constitute a legally binding restaurant contract, nor does it guarantee table availability.

4. Contact Information

For inquiries regarding this privacy disclosure, please visit our Contact Page or write to hello@pokebowl-freshfusion.sample.
