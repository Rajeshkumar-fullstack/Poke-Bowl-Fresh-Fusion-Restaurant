Goal Customization
Dietary Tips

Tailor any signature or custom bowl to meet your specific wellness targets:

  • Low Sodium: Request dressings on the side and choose Sweet Ponzu Lime over soy shoyu.
  • Ketogenic: Swap rice for mixed greens or cauliflower rice + double avocado.
  • Muscle Gain: Add double protein (Salmon + Ahi Tuna) for 50g+ clean protein.

Allergy Notification Procedure

If you or anyone in your party has a severe food allergy, please alert our team before placing your order enquiry:

  1. Our prep team changes gloves, uses sanitized cutting boards, and wipes down prep tables.
  2. Dedicated gluten-free tamari and separate utensils are designated for allergen orders.
  3. However, please note our open kitchen handles sesame, soy, seafood, and nuts, so 100% trace absence cannot be legally guaranteed.
